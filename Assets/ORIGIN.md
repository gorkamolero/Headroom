# Headroom artwork

The black disc and sixteen curved flame rays are adapted from Gorka Molero's Saga project, at his request. The editable contours come from Saga's traced black-sun-flame artwork. Headroom uses a new compact composition in vermilion, ink and warm ivory, plus monochrome template variants for the menu bar.

`black-sun-paths.json` contains the vector contours. `scripts/build-icons.py` produces the SVG, PNG, ICNS and PDF assets deterministically with librsvg and Apple's icon tools. No image-generation service was used for this adaptation. No artwork from Vivid is included.
